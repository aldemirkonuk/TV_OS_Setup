Maison launcher

One home screen, shaped like the one on your TV now: wallpaper, Wi-Fi and settings at the top right, rows of wide app cards. ↑/↓ moves between rows (Apps, Continue watching, Music, Home, Assistant, Photos), ←/→ moves along a row, Enter opens, Esc goes back. Press ↑ from the apps row to reach Search, Quick settings, Wi-Fi, Settings and your profile. Hold OK (or press Menu, or right-click) on an app or show for more options. Hold G, or the ✦ button on the remote, to open the Gemini pop-up, the way holding the assistant button will on the TV. Click the screen first, or use the remote in the corner.

Putting it on your TV

The launcher app project is in your files as maison-launcher. It's an Android TV app that shows this exact screen full-screen, fills the apps row with the apps actually installed on the stick, and opens them for real. Wi-Fi and Settings open the stick's own settings.

  1. Build the APK (one time): upload the folder to a GitHub repository. The included workflow builds maison-launcher.apk automatically under the Actions tab. Android Studio also works.
  2. Install it on the Mi TV Stick or Fire TV with the free Downloader app or adb install maison-launcher.apk.
  3. Make it the home screen: open Maison, go to Settings → "Make Maison my home screen", and turn on its Accessibility service. When the stock home screen appears, Maison opens over it, which is the same trick Projectivy uses.
